About Lesotho

Where the land touches the sky.

Lesotho is a small mountain kingdom completely surrounded by South Africa, and the only country in the world entirely above 1,000 metres. Its high peaks, winding roads and Basotho traditions make it one of Africa's most distinctive destinations.

  • Capital: Maseru
  • Languages: Sesotho and English
  • Currency: Loti (LSL)
  • Symbol of identity: the Basotho blanket and conical hat
